SMS Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Renovations Delivered LLC, operating as Renovations Delivered (RD), operates the employee receipt tool at rddashboard.com and the RD Receipt Alerts messaging program. This policy covers information handled for that program.

Information we collect

We collect information employees provide and information generated while processing company receipts, including:

  • Employee identity and contact information, including mobile phone numbers.
  • SMS consent, enrollment and opt-out records.
  • Card last four digits, charge descriptions, amounts and transaction dates.
  • Receipt photos or documents, job and accounting allocations, and explanations submitted with a receipt.
  • Submission, approval and correction history, along with messaging delivery and system records needed to operate and troubleshoot the program.

How information is used

We use this information to request and process company expense receipts, send receipt notices and requested links, support employees, maintain accounting records, and document consent and workflow actions.

Access and service providers

Receipt information is available to authorized company personnel according to their responsibilities. We use service providers to support messaging, email, file storage and accounting, including Twilio, Google, Dropbox and Sage as applicable. These providers may process the information needed to operate those services.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop program texts. You can continue submitting receipts through your dashboard login. Opting out of texts does not remove accounting records or the history needed to document consent and prior activity.

Contact qsieja@renovationsdelivered.com with privacy questions or requests concerning your information.
