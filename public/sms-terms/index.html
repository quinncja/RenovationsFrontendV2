RD Receipt Alerts

SMS Terms and Conditions

Effective September 29, 2026

RD Receipt Alerts is an employee messaging program operated by Renovations Delivered LLC, operating as Renovations Delivered (RD). These terms govern the receipt-alert program and its forms at rddashboard.com.

What the program sends

Employees who opt in receive automated texts related to company credit card receipts. Messages may include:

  • Purchase notifications and links to upload and categorize receipts.
  • A reminder when a receipt form remains incomplete after 24 hours.
  • Requests to correct a receipt or resubmit it when a related charge is added.
  • Receipt-form links requested by texting NEW RECEIPT.

This program is for employee expense administration and does not send marketing messages.

Frequency and timing

Message frequency varies with purchases, requests and corrections. We send one reminder per incomplete submission cycle. A correction request or reopened receipt begins a new cycle and may result in another reminder.

Reminders are sent between 6 a.m. and 6 p.m. Chicago time. Purchase notifications, correction requests and other receipt notices may arrive at any hour.

Consent and charges

Enrollment requires your explicit consent after the program and its disclosures have been explained to you. We record your consent and send an enrollment confirmation. Requesting a form does not, by itself, enroll you in recurring alerts.

Message and data rates may apply. Employees who do not want texts can submit receipts through their dashboard login.

Stopping messages and getting help

Reply STOP to opt out. You will receive an opt-out confirmation, after which program texts will stop. Previously enrolled employees may reply START to resume.

Reply HELP for help. You can also contact qsieja@renovationsdelivered.com.

Delivery

Message delivery may be delayed or unavailable. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Use your dashboard login to manage receipts if a text does not arrive.
